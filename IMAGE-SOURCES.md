## Image handling
- Images are stored locally under `public/images/products/`.
- WebP conversion quality: 86.
- Images are lazy-loaded in catalogue cards and the gallery.
- Original screenshot resolution is retained up to the supplied source dimensions; these are not true HD manufacturer originals.

## Customer-facing accuracy
Always ask Big City Gadgets for current photos of the exact pre-loved unit, its condition, storage and availability before purchase.
